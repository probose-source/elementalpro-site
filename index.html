FAQ

Common questions.

What does fractional mean?

A senior executive works with your business part-time, on a set schedule, for a monthly fee instead of a full-time salary.

How are we matched with a lead?

By the problem and the industry. The diagnostic call decides whether the work needs a COO, a CMO or both.

What do the AI agents do, and who checks them?

Agents handle research, drafting, content production, scheduling, reporting and process automation. The lead reviews every output, and regulated or client-facing work goes to a person before it ships.
